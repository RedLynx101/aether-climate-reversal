# Feasibility Gate Scorecard Notes

Last updated: 2026-06-09

This note is the synthesis layer for the current AETHER model suite. It does not create a new forecast. It turns the existing calculations into feasibility gates a scientist or advisor can evaluate.

## Current Read

AETHER is not ruled out by first-order physics. A 100 GtCO2/year gross system is large enough to more than offset current annual anthropogenic CO2 emissions if rebound and residual emissions are controlled. The stronger claim is conditional: the pathway becomes plausible only if several gates clear at once.

The current gate scorecard is deliberately blunt:

- Climate target arithmetic and the carbon-cycle management case are conditionally positive.
- Pathway potential depends on upper-tail deployment across several CDR pathways.
- Clean power, contactor/factory scale, durable storage, and delivered cost remain major bottlenecks.
- Robotics is conditionally useful but not yet proven at the level of task productivity.
- Uncertainty and governance are not afterthoughts. They decide whether the physical system becomes climate reversal or a license to keep emitting.

## Presentation Use

The scorecard should be used near the front of presentations and proposal drafts. It prevents two bad readings:

1. An overly dismissive reading where 100 GtCO2/year is treated as impossible because current DAC is tiny.
2. An overly optimistic reading where AI and robotics are treated as if they erase energy, storage, contactor, MRV, and governance floors.

The honest position is narrower and stronger: AETHER is a serious research program if it is framed as a coupled infrastructure problem with explicit go/no-go gates.
