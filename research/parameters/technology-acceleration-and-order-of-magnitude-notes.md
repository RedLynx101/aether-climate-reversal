# Technology Acceleration and Order-of-Magnitude Frontier

Last updated: 2026-06-09

This note answers a narrow question: if AETHER assumes powerful AI scientists, robotic labs, automated factories, and trillion-scale program spending, what order-of-magnitude cost collapse is actually required?

## Cost Frontier

| Scenario | Cost | Annual cost | Reduction vs current | Orders |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Current DAC-like | $605.56/tCO2 | $60.556T/year | 1x | 0 |
| AETHER automation push | $85.93/tCO2 | $8.593T/year | 7.05x | 0.848 |
| Moonshot modular | $40.33/tCO2 | $4.033T/year | 15.01x | 1.176 |
| Deep abundance floor | $24.17/tCO2 | $2.417T/year | 25.06x | 1.399 |

The current DAC-like stack is about $605.6/tCO2. The deep-abundance floor is about $24.2/tCO2. That is a 25.1x reduction, or about 1.40 orders of magnitude. It is large, but not a thousand-fold claim. The problem is that even after a 25x reduction, 100 GtCO2/year still costs about $2.4T/year under the current floor assumptions.

## Trillion-Scale RD&D Context

NCSES reports U.S. R&D performance at an estimated $939.6B in 2023. IEA estimates global public energy R&D near $55B in 2025 and corporate energy R&D near $160B in 2024. A trillion-dollar-per-year AETHER RD&D program would therefore be roughly comparable to all current U.S. R&D and several times larger than current public plus corporate energy R&D.

| Program | Annual budget | 20-year budget | Multiple of U.S. R&D | Multiple of public+corporate energy R&D |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AETHER RD&D program 100B/y | $100B/year | $2T | 0.11x | 0.47x |
| AETHER RD&D program 300B/y | $300B/year | $6T | 0.32x | 1.4x |
| AETHER RD&D program 1000B/y | $1000B/year | $20T | 1.06x | 4.65x |

This does not prove feasibility. It shows that the requested level of mobilization is not a normal climate-tech program. It is a national- or civilization-scale industrial program. If AI and robotics really create much cheaper scientific labor, laboratory throughput, construction labor, and factory output, the program might buy more effective experimentation per dollar than past programs. The model still treats that as an assumption until task-level productivity and R&D throughput are sourced.

## Hard Floors

The cost stack can fall by more than one order of magnitude only if multiple buckets move together:

- Energy cost must fall through lower energy intensity and cheaper clean power.
- Plant/contactors must move from bespoke projects to modular mass manufacturing.
- Sorbents, membranes, catalysts, and materials must improve through AI-assisted search and recycling.
- Storage must become a drilled, monitored, routinized infrastructure system rather than a bespoke project.
- MRV must get cheaper without becoming weaker.
- Finance and permitting must become standardized without deleting local consent or liability.

The deep-abundance case is therefore a coupled frontier, not a prediction. AETHER fails if any major bucket stays near current DAC-like values.
