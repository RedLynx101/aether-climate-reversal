## How To Use These Sources For Now

Use these as robotics-scaling leads, not as settled evidence.

Acceptable current wording:

> Reported 2026 social-media claims attributed to Figure CEO Brett Adcock suggested a rapid production ramp, including one humanoid per hour and a 250-robot month, but the original X posts need archival verification before citation.

Do not write:

> Figure Robotics produces one humanoid per hour, or produced 250 robots in a month.

Those claims may become usable if verified through archives, screenshots, official Figure statements, or corroborating primary sources. Until then, use them only as scenario calibration.

## Why It Matters

If humanoid and industrial robot production can ramp by orders of magnitude, robotics becomes relevant to climate reversal in two ways:

- Direct labor substitution for construction, logistics, maintenance, inspection, and plant operation.
- Indirect cost compression through automated factories, mining, drilling, material handling, and monitoring.

The current AETHER transition model annualizes the 250/month lead to 3,000 robots/year only for comparison. It shows that even a 250/month single-company run rate remains far below high-end AETHER robot-fleet requirements.

The paper should still avoid humanoid tunnel vision. Climate reversal infrastructure may depend more on specialized industrial robots than on general humanoids.
