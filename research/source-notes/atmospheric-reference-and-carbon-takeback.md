# Dated observation and policy precedent

Accessed 2026-09-05. NOAA GML's global monthly series reported preliminary May 2026 CO2 of 428.73 ppm, updated 2026-08-05. This is a global dry-air mole fraction, not the Mauna Loa station series and not a live feed. The public JSON records the date and source. Percentage of dry air is ppm / 10,000; the ratio to the approximate 280 ppm reference is 428.73 / 280. Concentration differences do not specify total removal requirements because land and ocean reservoirs respond.

Source: https://www.gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/global.html

Jenkins et al. (2021), *Upstream decarbonization through a carbon takeback obligation: An affordable backstop climate policy*, Joule 5(11), 2777–2796, is prior work on requiring storage associated with fossil-carbon supply. It is a relevant mechanism to compare with AETHER, not proof that AETHER's institutional or financing assumptions work.
