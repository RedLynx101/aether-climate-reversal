# Code of Conduct

AETHER depends on adversarial review without personal hostility. Participants should challenge claims, methods, incentives, and evidence directly while treating contributors with respect.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate misrepresentation of evidence, undisclosed fabrication, and sustained disruption of technical discussion. Good-faith disagreement, including forceful criticism of the project's central premise, is welcome.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The owner may remove content, restrict participation, or document a moderation decision when needed to protect the research process.
