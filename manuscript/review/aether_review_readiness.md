# AETHER evidence and review readiness

Last updated: 2026-08-10

The current paper is a serious working research artifact, not a journal submission. It has a source register, BibTeX bibliography, parameter-evidence map, equation ledger, generated model suite, calculation audit, claim/evidence matrix, inspected figures, internal adversarial review, and a twelve-gate submission ledger.

## What reviewers can inspect

- Source claims: `references/source-register.md` and `references/bibtex/sources.bib`
- Model assumptions: `research/parameters/` and `research/assumptions/`
- Equations and units: `analysis/tables/aether_model_equation_ledger.csv` and `analysis/tables/aether_dimensioned_unit_checks.csv`
- Claim classes: `analysis/tables/aether_manuscript_claim_evidence_matrix.csv`
- Calculation audit: `analysis/tables/aether_independent_calculation_audit.csv`
- Feasibility gates: `analysis/tables/aether_feasibility_gate_scorecard.csv`
- Submission gates: `analysis/tables/aether_submission_readiness_gates.csv`
- Adversarial tests: `manuscript/review/aether_adversarial_review_plan.md`
- Current paper: `manuscript/submission/AETHER_Atmospheric_Engineering_Through_High_Energy_Removal_v0.45.pdf`

Claims are classified as source-backed, source-backed plus derived, model output, scenario assumption, sensitivity result, governance hypothesis, or speculation. Reviewers should challenge the source, transformation, and scenario separately.

## Current gate result

Five gates pass: citation coverage, figure resolution, equation reproducibility, claim/evidence mapping, and duplicate/style screening.

Five gates remain partial: clean-power delivery, storage/MRV/lifecycle treatment, robotics field productivity, adversarial specialist review, and final submission format.

Two gates fail: publication-grade climate modeling and species-level emissions inputs. The forcing-driven FAIR run is diagnostic because aggregate forcing placeholders, missing species trajectories, spin-up, zero-emissions commitment, lifecycle traces, and uncertainty ensembles still prevent publication-grade climate claims.

## Appropriate use

The paper can be shared with scientists, engineers, policy researchers, and careful generalists for criticism. Its strongest value is that it turns a broad “what if” into coupled constraints and falsification gates.

It should not be cited as evidence that AETHER will work, that its AI/robotics premise will occur on schedule, or that its 100 GtCO2/year boundary is a plausible forecast. The honest status is stronger than a brainstorm and weaker than a submission.
