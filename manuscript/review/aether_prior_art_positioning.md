# AETHER Prior Art and Contribution Boundary

Last updated: 2026-06-09

This note prevents a weak version of AETHER: the version where the paper acts as if carbon dioxide removal, direct air capture, atmospheric governance, or AI materials discovery are new ideas. They are not.

AETHER's strongest contribution is the coupling. The paper asks whether a future AI- and robotics-accelerated physical economy could move enough energy, materials, machinery, storage, monitoring, and governance capacity to make 100 GtCO2/year removal plausible. That is different from claiming that AETHER invented the underlying pathways.

The positioning matrix is `analysis/tables/aether_prior_art_positioning_matrix.csv`. The current conclusion is cautious: AETHER should present itself as an integrated feasibility-boundary and abundance-scenario paper, not as a claim of first invention. The novelty, if the project earns that word later, will come from the combined model: AI/robotics acceleration, clean-energy buildout, air-contactor hardware, storage-state conversion, pathway portfolio, uncertainty, rebound, and commons governance evaluated together at 100 GtCO2/year.

The best skeptical papers are not obstacles to cite defensively. They are constraints AETHER has to survive.
