# AETHER internal peer-review pass: v0.46

**Review type:** AI-assisted internal editorial and technical review. This is not external disciplinary peer review, engineering certification, or validation of the research program.

## Judgment

The revision now has a defensible principal claim: AETHER is a conditional research program for an accountable carbon-removal service, centered on a reproducible regional analytical case and explicit long-term institutional requirements. It no longer presents the old broad screening package as evidence that a 100 GtCO2/year system can be built by 2046.

The working paper is suitable for bounded expert feedback on its methods and research design. It is not ready to support a viability claim, a deployment recommendation, a calibrated climate forecast, or an externally reviewed scientific conclusion.

## What was checked

- The principal paper and proposal have been consolidated around public-carbon-service architecture, a regional 1 MtCO2/year analytical benchmark, and stated failure conditions.
- Readable manuscript citations use author-year hyperlinks rather than raw bibliography keys; the links were selected from the existing source register.
- The 100 GtCO2/year number is labelled gross, extreme, and non-normative throughout the new material.
- The text distinguishes gross capture, durable physical accounting, crediting, and net climate result; it keeps heat, electricity, and chemical energy distinct.
- The storage-boundary defect, fixed carbon-baseline defect, partially coupled integration boundary, hand-set Monte Carlo distributions, and unrealized-capacity learning defect are disclosed.
- Institutional alternatives are compared by control and incentive mechanisms, rather than being treated as settled political conclusions.

## Material corrections reflected in v0.46

1. **Representative-day storage.** The legacy daily dispatch annualized initial battery energy. The cyclic-storage diagnostic reduces the named upper-tail case from 121.97 to approximately 98.40 GtCO2/year. The corrected result remains synthetic and non-8760-hour.
2. **Carbon baseline.** The former fixed-concentration construction is withdrawn from atmospheric-forecast use. The first RCMIP/Joos replacement is quarantined after an implausible zero-future-emissions diagnostic; it must resolve reservoir initialization before being called a hybrid conditional baseline.
3. **Integration.** The current integrated model is labelled partially coupled. It does not establish that regional dispatch, lifecycle/MRV, field productivity, storage, and cost assumptions hold simultaneously for one shared deployment.
4. **Lifecycle and crediting.** Retained gross and lifecycle CO2e debit are separated; credit buffers follow physical accounting. Scalar ledgers remain unsuitable for time- and species-resolved climate claims.
5. **Learning.** Learning now follows lagged modeled realized removal rather than planned capacity. The initial reference learning state is an unsourced screening proxy and is labelled as such.
6. **Uncertainty.** Monte Carlo pass shares and correlated families are model behavior under hand-set assumptions, not probabilities or expected outcomes.

## Remaining review gates

| Reviewer domain | Decisive question | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon cycle and climate | Does a paired, historically contextualized workflow isolate removal's marginal effect? | Absolute output is quarantined; reservoir initialization, species-emissions, and calibrated climate work remain open. |
| Process and power | Does a named 1 MtCO2/year process receive required additional power and heat under 8760-hour constraints? | Representative-day diagnostic only. |
| Storage and MRV | Can a named basin support injection, monitoring, liability, and credit buffers? | Regional/proxy work only. |
| Automation | Does automation change a measured bottleneck under comparable physical conditions? | Research question; no field validation. |
| Finance and governance | Can legacy drawdown and long-tail liabilities be funded as emissions revenue declines? | Institutional proposition and arithmetic examples only. |

## Publication boundary

External feedback should be solicited as a request to falsify or improve a limited artifact, not to endorse the entire project. The first packet should contain the regional case inputs and code, the carbon-baseline diagnostic and proposed repair, the storage conservation test, the lifecycle/MRV ledger, and the stock-flow finance model. A failed gate must alter the associated claim.
