# Intellectual-property and publication note

Last reviewed: 2026-08-10

This repository is structured to keep authorship and provenance visible, not to claim exclusive ownership of every underlying idea.

## What the licenses accomplish

- Apache License 2.0 permits reuse of the code while preserving copyright and NOTICE attribution and adding an explicit contributor patent grant for covered code.
- CC BY 4.0 permits reuse of original research content while requiring appropriate credit, a license link, and disclosure of changes.
- `CITATION.cff`, `AUTHORS.md`, `NOTICE`, releases, and contribution history create a durable public attribution trail naming Noah Hicks as project originator and principal author.

## What they do not accomplish

Copyright protects original expression fixed in a tangible medium. It generally does not protect ideas, procedures, processes, systems, methods of operation, concepts, principles, or discoveries. An open license therefore cannot make the underlying AETHER concepts exclusive to Noah Hicks.

No separate patent strategy is part of the current release. The project is being opened for review under its stated software and content licenses. That decision does not turn the underlying concepts into exclusive property.

## Release recommendation

The repository is suitable to share as a conditional working paper and open research program. It should not be marketed as externally peer reviewed, publication-grade climate modeling, or a validated 100 GtCO2/year deployment plan.
