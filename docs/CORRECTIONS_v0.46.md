# AETHER v0.46 corrections and replacement notice

**Date:** September 2026

**Applies to:** the earlier AETHER working-paper and proposal model narrative

## What is being replaced

The prior long-form AETHER paper and proposal are superseded as the current presentation of the research. Their historical files remain available for reproducibility, but their numerical summaries should not be reused as current scientific evidence without the correction and the stated model boundary.

This is not a cosmetic revision or a claim that two isolated gates were fixed. The earlier package combined transparent and sometimes useful screens with presentation that overstated how much had been integrated and validated. v0.46 narrows the claim to a conditional public-carbon-service research program and one bounded regional analytical case.

## Specific corrections

### Storage accounting

The legacy regional dispatch calculation initialized storage part-charged, simulated one representative day, and annualized it without requiring an equal terminal state of charge. It therefore reused initial stored energy. A cyclic fixed-point correction using the same synthetic daily profiles changes the reported named cases from 15.46, 47.93, 64.26, 121.97, and 10.19 GtCO2/year to approximately 9.87, 33.47, 42.72, 98.40, and 3.62 GtCO2/year. The prior statement that the upper-tail dispatch case clears 100 GtCO2/year is withdrawn.

The corrected calculation is still a representative-day diagnostic, not an 8760-hour regional power model, a weather-resolved result, or a deployment forecast.

### Carbon and climate treatment

The old carbon screen added future pulse responses to a fixed contemporary concentration. It had no historical reservoir initialization or evolving baseline; in a zero-future-emissions diagnostic its concentration remained flat through 2100. Absolute concentration and temperature paths derived from that construction are withdrawn from evidentiary use.

The replacement work uses a published evolving RCMIP reference with a Joos impulse-response perturbation and matched controls. Its first absolute-output method is quarantined after an implausible zero-future-emissions diagnostic under large off-reference anomalies: concentration fell and then rose from 426.582160 to 433.368307 ppm. No absolute ppm path, temperature path, or target-date climate claim from that method is current evidence. It must resolve historical reservoir initialization before it can be labelled a hybrid conditional baseline; it would still not constitute a calibrated Earth-system spin-up, a validated absolute forecast, or a resolution of non-CO2 forcing and attribution.

### Integration and uncertainty

The legacy integrated screen did not ingest later regional dispatch, lifecycle/MRV, field-productivity, and other upgraded screens. A favorable row from separate model families did not prove that one common scenario could satisfy their shared power, land, materials, transport, storage, capital, and time constraints. Current integrated results are therefore labelled **partially coupled screening**.

The legacy Monte Carlo pass shares used hand-set distributions. They are not probabilities of real-world success, expected outcomes, or confidence intervals. Correlated scenario families changed both dependence and marginal assumptions, so their favorable results cannot be credited to correlation alone.

### Lifecycle, learning, and economics

The prior lifecycle formula combined retention and lifecycle debit in a way that could reduce the debit when retention worsened. v0.46 separates retained gross from lifecycle CO2e debit and then applies a separate crediting layer. The net-after-lifecycle ledger is signed, so a negative result remains visible as a net burden; only credit issuance is floored at zero. This remains a simplified annual accounting convention, not a species- and time-resolved climate calculation.

The prior learning curve used planned capacity, which granted learning to an unbuilt schedule. v0.46 uses lagged cumulative modeled realized removal. Its initial learning state is an explicit screening proxy, not a claim about historical global deployment. Cost floors and many cost inputs remain scenario assumptions.

The paper's financial examples are only arithmetic. They do not establish a tax, tariff, funding plan, or economic forecast. A durable service must separately model operating costs, capital, legacy drawdown, monitoring, reversal reserves, and declining emissions-fee revenue.

## What remains valid but limited

The earlier work can still support transparent order-of-magnitude calculations, a useful inventory of constraints, and a research agenda. It does not establish that 100 GtCO2/year is physically, financially, socially, or politically viable. Approximately 280 ppm remains a preindustrial reference range, not a demonstrated modern optimum. AI scenarios remain context for timing and research attention, not evidence of physical automation productivity.

## Replacement standard

Future results must identify a scenario contract with location, year, removal process, gross/durable/credit/net accounting layer, source or assumption for each material input, shared-resource constraints, and reproducible outputs. Claims should be promoted only after the relevant carbon, power, storage, lifecycle/MRV, automation, and financial gates are independently reviewable.
