## Source Discipline

- The prior abundance memo is context, not evidence.
- Company or social-media robotics claims can motivate scenarios, but the manuscript should not treat them as established facts unless archived or independently verified.
- Robot unit costs in the model are explicit assumptions. The paper should report sensitivity to those assumptions rather than claiming current humanoid prices.
- CO2 splitting energy is a thermodynamic floor, not an engineering forecast. Real systems require additional energy and capital.
- Simple ppm drawdown calculations in the model are atmosphere-only bookkeeping and do not include carbon-cycle rebound from land and ocean reservoirs.
